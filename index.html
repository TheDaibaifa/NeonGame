🔗 NEON TETHER (2P)

Two neon spheres bound by an elastic energy tether. Swing your opponent into the pulsing spike boundary to score. The tether snaps at maximum tension, giving 2 seconds of free movement before reforming.

  • P1 (Cyan): WASD to move | Space Polarity Flip
  • P2 (Pink): ▲◀▼▶ Arrows | Enter Polarity Flip

Polarity Flip: Reverses the tether pull for 1 second. Costs 50 energy. Watch the Tension Meter — when full, the tether SNAPS! First to 3 spike hits wins.

⚔️ CYBER SLICE (2P)

A strategic duel where every shot leaves a permanent laser wall. Navigate the maze of neon beams, trap your opponent, and use Shatter to clear the board when cornered.

  • P1 (Cyan): WASD to move | Space Shoot / Charge Shatter
  • P2 (Pink): ▲◀▼▶ Arrows | Enter Shoot / Charge Shatter

Rules: Hitting your own wall is safe. Hitting an enemy wall stuns you and deals 1 damage. Direct hits also deal 1 damage. Hold Action for 1s to Shatter all walls (uses energy). First to win 3 rounds wins.

💣 NEON CHAOS BALL (3-4P)

High-speed Hot Potato! The ball constantly changes colors. If it's your color, catch it safely and throw it! If not, touching it hurts you and speeds up the explosion timer.

  • P1 (Cyan): WASD | Space Launch
  • P2 (Pink): ▲◀▼▶ | Enter Launch
  • P3 (Yellow): IJKL | U Launch
  • P4 (Green): TFGH | Y Launch

Rules: Avoid the ball if it isn't your color or if it's White (Neutral). Timer ticks down from 30s. When it reaches 0, the closest player explodes! Last player standing wins.

🜔 SYMMETRY CLASH (3-4P)

Sumo push on a shrinking hexagon. You cannot move freely; you are anchored to the edges. Pivot around the perimeter and shoot shockwaves to blast opponents into the void!

  • P1 (Cyan): AD pivot | W or Space Pulse
  • P2 (Pink): ◀▶ pivot | ▲ or Enter Pulse
  • P3 (Yellow): JL pivot | I or U Pulse
  • P4 (Green): FH pivot | T or Y Pulse

Rules: Firing a pulse pushes you backward. When pulses collide in the center, they create a massive outward Symmetry Burst. Watch your footing as the arena shrinks over time!
